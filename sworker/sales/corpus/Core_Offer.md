# Core Offer

## Offer Name

Sovereign AI Workflow & Knowledge Systems Audit

## Fee

$3,500 flat (two-week audit), with implementation scoped and priced separately after the audit findings are reviewed with the client.

## What it is

A practical audit, not a slide deck. We inventory the workflows that burn employee time, identify which ones can be automated with systems the client *owns*, and produce a prioritized build plan with effort and expected return for each.

## Target Customer

Mid-market and SMB businesses (10+ employees) that already feel the weight of repetitive administrative work, expensive SaaS, or fragmented internal knowledge — and would rather own their AI infrastructure than rent it from a cloud provider that ingests their data.

## Follow-on offers (scoped after audit)

- Local-first AI assistant (internal knowledge + research)
- Workflow automation build
- Document-processing / intake automation
- Private RAG knowledge system
- SaaS-replacement automation
- Ongoing advisory / retainer
